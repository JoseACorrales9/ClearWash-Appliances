PRODUCT TECHNOLOGY

Inside a ClearWash Machine

Motor Control

Controls drum speed and direction throughout the wash cycle.

Water Management

Coordinates valves, water levels, pumps, drainage, and temperature.

Sensors

Monitors operating conditions for reliability and safe operation.

Embedded Software

Controls cycle timing, warnings, machine behavior, and user-interface communication.

Child Lock

Keeps the washing machine locked during operation to prevent children from opening it.

Automatic Load Sensing

Detects the size of the load and adjusts water levels and cycle settings.

Adjustable Water Temperature

Allows users to select the appropriate water temperature for different types of laundry.

Clear Cycle Status Indicators

Shows cycle progress, time remaining, and important washer alerts.

Easy-to-Use Controls

Provides a simple and clear control layout for easier operation.

Safety & Fault Detection

Monitors the washer for potential problems and alerts the user when an issue is detected
