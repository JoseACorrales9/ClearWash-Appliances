HOW WE OPERATE

ClearWash Company Operations

Producing a modern washing machine requires coordination between engineering, manufacturing, software, testing, customer support, and distribution.

BUSINESS OPERATIONS

Core Departments

01

Product Development & Engineering

Engineers design the mechanical, electrical, electronic, and software systems used throughout ClearWash washing machines.

02

Manufacturing

Manufacturing teams assemble machine frames, motors, drums, water systems, electronics, controls, and exterior components into finished appliances.

03

Software & Controls

Embedded software manages cycle timing, sensors, motors, pumps, water valves, safety systems, diagnostics, and communication with the user controls.

04

Quality Assurance

Quality assurance personnel test washing machines for reliability, washing performance, electrical safety, correct operation, and manufacturing quality.

05

Customer Service

Customer service personnel assist users with operating questions, troubleshooting, warranties, replacement parts, and repair coordination.

06

Sales & Marketing

Sales and marketing teams promote ClearWash products and coordinate distribution through appliance retailers, home improvement stores, online stores, and regional distributors.

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

From Design to Customer

A washing machine passes through several stages before reaching the customer.

1. Design

Engineers develop mechanical systems, electronics, controls, and software.

2. Prototype

Early versions are built and evaluated for operation, usability, safety, and performance.

3. Testing

Machines are tested under repeated and abnormal operating conditions before production.

4. Manufacturing

Approved designs are produced, inspected, packaged, and distributed to retailers.

QUALITY & RELIABILITY

Testing Before Production

Washing machines combine moving mechanical parts, electrical systems, water systems, sensors, electronics, and software. A failure in any of these areas can affect product reliability or safety.

ClearWash evaluates product designs throughout the development process rather than relying only on final inspection.

Testing may include motor operation, water filling and draining, vibration, temperature monitoring, lid or door locking, user controls, electronic sensors, diagnostic messages, and abnormal operating conditions.

Information from testing, customer support, warranty claims, and service records is also used to identify opportunities for future product improvements.
