PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

From Design to Customer

Our washing machines pass through several stages before reaching you.

1. Design

Engineers develop the mechanical systems, electronics, controls, and software that go into our machines.

2. Prototype

Early versions are built and thoroughly planned to maximize its performance, usability, reliability safety.

3. Testing

Machines are tested under repeated and extreme operating conditions to met our saftey standards.

4. Manufacturing

Completed designs are then produced and inspected before being sent to retailers.

QUALITY & RELIABILITY

Testing Before Production

Our washing machines combine moving mechanical parts, electrical systems, water systems, sensors, electronics, and software. We test for failure in any of these areas that can affect our products reliability and/or safety.

ClearWash evaluates product designs throughout the entire development process rather than just a final product inspection.

Our testing includes motor operation, water filling and draining, vibration, temperature monitoring, lid and/or door locking, user controls, electronic sensors, diagnostic messages, and different operating conditions.

The information from testing, customer support, warranty claims, and our service records are used to identify future product improvement opportunities.
