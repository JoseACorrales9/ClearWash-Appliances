CURRENT BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Our machines work.
Our controls need improvement.

ClearWash has received customer feedback indicating that the controls on its current washing machines can be confusing and difficult to understand.

CUSTOMER FEEDBACK

Where Users Are Having Difficulty

Several recurring usability concerns have been identified through customer feedback and support interactions.

01

Cycle Selection

Some customers are unsure which wash cycle is appropriate for a particular type of laundry.

02

Machine Status

Users may have difficulty determining what stage of the wash cycle the machine is currently performing.

03

Settings

The relationship between wash cycles and available options can be difficult for some customers to understand.

04

Error Messages

Warning codes and error messages may not clearly explain what has happened or what action the customer should take.

BUSINESS IMPACT

Why the Problem Matters

Confusing controls can result in incorrect cycle selections, unnecessary cycle restarts, customer frustration, and reduced satisfaction with the appliance.

Customers may also contact technical support for questions that could otherwise be resolved through clearer product operation and feedback.

These concerns can affect brand perception, customer loyalty, support costs, and the overall competitiveness of ClearWash products.

Different Users Have Different Needs

The current difficulty may affect customers differently depending on technical experience, age, vision, mobility, dexterity, or other accessibility considerations.

Broad Usability Concern

The issue is not limited to one button or one washing cycle. ClearWash must consider how customers interact with the machine as a complete system.

TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS

The Interface Is Part of a Larger System

Electronics

The user controls interact with the washing machine's existing electronic control system.

Sensors

Machine operation depends on information from water, temperature, load, safety, and other sensors.

Embedded Software

Cycle logic, timing, diagnostics, and machine behavior are controlled through embedded programming.

Safety

Any future changes must continue to support safe, reliable appliance operation.

OUTSIDE EXPERTISE

Why ClearWash Needs a Vendor

Addressing the current problem requires expertise across several technical and design disciplines.

ClearWash has internal experience with washing machine engineering and manufacturing, but the company does not currently have enough specialized experience across every area involved in evaluating and improving the overall user interaction.

Relevant areas include user-interface design, accessibility, embedded programming, electronics, usability testing, reliability, and appliance safety.

ClearWash is therefore interested in working with qualified outside suppliers capable of researching the current usability concerns and presenting possible approaches.

Suppliers are encouraged to consider different types of users, technical constraints, reliability, manufacturability, accessibility, and safety when evaluating the problem.

OPEN-ENDED PROJECT

We are defining the problem, not prescribing the answer.

ClearWash is not specifying a particular redesign at this stage. Prospective suppliers are encouraged to investigate the identified usability issues and present creative, well-supported approaches that meet the company's operational, technical, accessibility, reliability, and safety needs.
