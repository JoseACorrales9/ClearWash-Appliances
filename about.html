WHO WE ARE

About ClearWash

Company Description

ClearWash Appliances is a household appliance company that focuses on residential washing machines that are reliable and easy to use.

ClearWash was created with the idea that appliances should make everyday tasks easier without making customers deal with complicated controls or confusing features.

The company focuses on families, homeowners, apartment residents, first-time appliance buyers, older adults, and customers with different levels of experience using technology.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop reliable residential appliances that combine reliable effective performance with straightforward user operation, with a focus on accessibility, efficiency, and safety.

Brand Values

Simplicity Reliability Accessibility Safety Efficiency
