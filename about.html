WHO WE ARE

About ClearWash

A fictional appliance company created by Team 3.

Company Description

ClearWash Appliances is a household appliance company specializing in residential washing machines designed to provide dependable cleaning performance while remaining easy for customers to operate.

ClearWash was created around the idea that household appliances should make everyday tasks easier rather than require customers to understand complicated controls or technical terminology.

The company serves families, homeowners, apartment residents, first-time appliance buyers, older adults, and customers with different levels of technical experience.

Our Mission

To develop reliable residential appliances that combine effective performance with straightforward operation, accessibility, efficiency, and safety.

Brand Values

Simplicity Reliability Accessibility Safety Efficiency

DESIGN PRIORITIES

Accessibility

01

Vision

Controls and information should be understandable for users with different levels of vision.

02

Dexterity

Appliance operation should consider users with different levels of hand strength and mobility.

03

Understanding

Users should not require extensive technical knowledge to perform common household tasks.

04

Feedback

Users need understandable information about what the appliance is currently doing.
